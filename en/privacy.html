Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-10-16

Junmo Kim (the "Operator") takes care of your personal information when providing daisy, and works to comply with applicable laws (including Japan's and Korea's personal information protection acts).

1. Information we collect

Sign-up and sign-in

  • Email address, sign-in method (Apple / Google / LINE / email), and the unique identifier passed by your social sign-in provider
  • New beta accounts use social sign-in only. Passwords for existing email test accounts are stored as hashes, not plaintext.

Profile

  • Username, display name, bio, profile photo

Content you post

  • Outfit photos, text, closet photos and clothing details (name, type, brand, material, season, size and so on)
  • Comments, likes, saves, follows, blocks, reports, and Daisy requests and replies
  • Beta feedback (during the beta): the kind and text of your feedback, any screenshots you attach, and the app version, device model, OS and language. Only the operator sees it, and it is deleted when you delete your account.

Usage records

  • Activity records used to improve recommendations (for example likes, saves, detail views) and the taste profile built from them
  • Device token for push notifications, notification settings, app language
  • App error information: when the app stops or hits an error, the device model and state, OS and app version, when and where in the code it happened, the error details, and diagnostic installation and session identifiers. We do not add your daisy account ID, photos, posts or comments to this diagnostic information.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: used only when you take or choose a photo. We do not collect location or contacts.

Past conversations

  • Chat is not offered right now, but earlier conversations are kept. They are deleted when you delete your account.

2. How we use it

  • Creating accounts, signing in, verifying identity
  • Providing the Service: sharing outfits, managing your closet, friend features
  • Recognizing clothes in photos (AI) and filling in details
  • Improving feed and explore recommendations
  • Delivering push notifications
  • Handling reports, preventing misuse, security, and answering inquiries
  • Improving the Service and checking for errors

3. Service providers and transfers abroad

To run the Service we use or share information with the following providers.

  • Supabase — database, sign-in and photo storage. Account, profile and content data. Since October 10, 2026, Tokyo, Japan is our primary server location. Data on the previous Singapore server temporarily remain for migration verification and recovery. After 14 days of stability checks, we will review their deletion and update this notice once deletion is complete. The company is based in the United States.
  • Google (Gemini API) — recognizing clothes in photos, suggesting details, and creating features used for search and recommendations. The photo and clothing details you ask us to analyze. Processed in the United States and other countries.
  • Google Firebase / Apple — delivering push notifications. Device token and notification text.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — diagnosing app errors. The app error information above. Processed in the United States and elsewhere. Error records and related identifiers are kept for 90 days, after which deletion from live systems and backups begins.
  • Apple / Google / LINE — social sign-in. Each provider passes an identifier and, when available, an email address. LINE may not provide an email address.

Your information may be transferred to servers outside Japan and Korea. The Operator manages such transfers to keep your information safe.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties unless required by law or with your consent. We do not do advertising tracking or collect advertising identifiers.

5. Retention and deletion

  • We keep your information while you use the Service.
  • If you tap Settings > Delete account, your account is paused for 7 days (you can cancel during that time). After 7 days we delete your account, profile, photos and content you posted, closet, conversations, push tokens and so on. Deleted information cannot be restored.
  • If the law requires us to keep certain information, we keep it separately only for that period.
  • Server backups may retain data for a short time.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, stop its use, or withdraw consent. You can edit your profile and delete your account in the app; for other requests please contact us below. We respond without delay after verifying your identity.

7. About audience settings

Content and profiles you post publicly are visible to other users. Close-friends posts are visible only to people you added as close friends, and "Only me" is visible only to you. Profile photos are stored as public.

Outfits you post publicly may appear to other users as "similar outfit" recommendations. Close-friends and "Only me" posts are never recommended to other users, but may be recommended to you.

8. Minors

People under 18 can't sign up. If we learn we have collected information from someone under 18, we will delete it without delay.

9. Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access limits (row-level security so people can reach only their own data), and separate storage of secret keys. No method is perfectly safe, but we work to prevent incidents and respond quickly.

10. Changes

If we change this policy we will tell you in the app at least 7 days before it takes effect (30 days for changes unfavorable to you).
