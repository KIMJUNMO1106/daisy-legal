Community Guidelines

daisy is a place to browse each other's outfits and share inspiration. To keep it comfortable for everyone, please follow these guidelines.

Please do

  • Share your outfits and closet freely, and react kindly to others.
  • If other people are in your photo, check that they're okay with it first.
  • Double-check the audience (Everyone / Close friends / Only me) before you post.

What you can't post

  • Harassment and hate: insults, threats or bullying about someone's looks, body, gender, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation and similar
  • Sexual content: explicit nudity or sexual content. Sexual content involving minors is removed immediately and reported to the authorities when needed.
  • Violence and danger: violent or graphic content, content encouraging self-harm, illegal activity
  • Impersonation and deception: pretending to be another person or brand, or deceiving others
  • Violating others' rights: photos of others taken without consent, exposing personal information, using someone else's work without permission
  • Spam: promotional flooding, repeated advertising, automated accounts

Reporting and blocking

  • If a post, comment or user bothers you, you can report or block them. When you block someone, you can't see each other's posts.
  • The Operator reviews reports. Content that breaks the guidelines is hidden or removed, and repeated or serious cases can lead to account restrictions. We don't tell the other person who reported them.
  • If there is an urgent risk we may act first.

Appeals

If you think an action was a mistake, contact us below and we will look again.
